## 1. Voice fingerprint

- Sentence rhythm: Warm, grounded, human, and purposeful. Combines clear medium-length statements with occasional reflective questions and concrete dollar figures ($18.99/mo, $26.99/mo, up to 7 family members, 37 covered acute medications, $0 visit fees).
- Signature openers: Direct observations grounded in real family or community reality ("CEDEXX was built on a simple truth: millions of families delay care because traditional healthcare is expensive, inconvenient, or inaccessible when needed most.") or number-first hooks ($18.99/month for a family of 7 vs. $175 at a walk-in clinic).
- Punctuation habits: Clean punctuation; at most 1 em dash per 100 words; zero robotic staccato fragments ("Short. Punchy. Done.").
- Words and phrases you use a lot: "Better Care. Here. Now.", "wherever people live, learn, work, and thrive", "whole person: mind, body, and spirit", "with intention and care", "at a fraction of traditional costs", "multifamily communities, student housing, educational institutions, and school districts".
- Words and phrases you NEVER use: "delve", "tapestry", "game-changer", "deep dive", "leverage", "in today's fast-paced world", "The result?", "Plot twist:", "Let me be honest", "Stop X, start Y".
- Emoji: Zero emojis in formal LinkedIn posts and blog articles; minimal, intentional use (1-2 max) only on Instagram Reels.
- Formatting: 1-2 sentence paragraphs separated by double line breaks; 0-2 hashtags at the very end on LinkedIn; external URLs placed in the first comment on LinkedIn.

## 3. Hard rules (always / never)

- Always:
  - Include at least 1 odd-precision real number ($18.99/mo, $26.99/mo, 37 acute medications, 7 family members, $0 copay).
  - Include the mandatory compliance disclosure when describing clinical services: "CEDEXX is a technology and wellness membership platform powered by Lyric Health, not a doctor or healthcare provider."
  - Close LinkedIn posts with a genuine, specific question for property leaders, employers, or families.
- Never:
  - Never open a LinkedIn post with a question (lead with a number or concrete statement).
  - Never put external links inside the LinkedIn post body (always place `www.cedexx.net` or `www.cedexx.net/partners` in the First Comment).
  - Never use AI-slop reveal bridges or fake vulnerability framing.
