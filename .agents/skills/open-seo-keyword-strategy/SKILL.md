# OpenSEO Keyword & Content Strategy (CEDEXX)

Use this skill alongside the technical `seo` skill to grow organic search traffic for `www.cedexx.net`.

## Core Keyword Pillars for CEDEXX
1. **Affordable Family Telehealth (B2C):** `affordable virtual urgent care family`, `telehealth no insurance needed`, `$0 copay online doctor`, `virtual doctor visit with prescription`.
2. **College Student & No-ID Telehealth:** `telehealth for college students`, `online counseling for college exam stress`, `healthcare membership no ID required`.
3. **Multifamily & Employer Wellness Amenities (B2B):** `multifamily resident wellness amenities`, `telehealth amenity for apartment residents`, `affordable healthcare benefits for hourly employees`, `student housing wellness program`.

## Optimization Checklist for New Blog Posts & Landing Pages
- Map each article or page to 1 primary search intent + 3 long-tail semantic queries.
- Include natural, human-written headings (`H1`, `H2`, `H3`) without AI cliches or robotic phrasing.
- Interlink blog posts (`/blog`) directly to conversion pages (`/enroll`, `/pricing`, `/partners`, `/about`).
